I am a pre-doctoral research associate at Duke University's Nicholas School of the Environment, working with Professors Yichun Fan and Martin Smith. I hold a M.A. in Political Economy from Duke University and a B.A. in Economics and Agricultural Economics from Renmin University of China.

My research lies at the intersection of **political economy and environmental economics**, with a focus on how institutions and social incentives shape the management of natural resources and long-run development. I combine **causal inference with dynamic and computational modeling**, including system dynamics, optimal control, game theory, and agent-based models.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 entry. Feel free to reach out at [qingwen.deng@duke.edu](mailto:qingwen.deng@duke.edu).
